# Changelog

All notable changes to this mod are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) (while the version starts with 0, anything can change between releases).

When a version is released, its section is renamed from `[Unreleased]` to `[x.y.z] - date` (see [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md)).

## [Unreleased]

First public preview. Start a **new world**: the Satisfy dimension and its places are only generated in new worlds.

### Added
- **VR capsule and two worlds.** Connect to Satisfy from a capsule; the real world and Satisfy are separate characters with their own inventory, Ender chest, health and food. Safe logout with a countdown. Three capsule tiers (Basic, Advanced, Diamond) with licenses, and a "rested" XP bonus with the Diamond one.
- **Fatigue.** Energy that drains while connected and recovers in the real world, with an on-screen bar, warnings and a safety protocol when it runs out.
- **Levels, stats, titles and skills**, with profile, quest and journal screens, hidden classes and rumors told by NPCs.
- **Blacksmithing.** Smelting and forging minigames, hammers, new metals (jaffa, mithril, blue orichalcum) and equipment with ratings from Normal to Myth and rolled options.
- **Bairan and Winston.** The starting village with Smith's quest chain, the North End Cave with Count Ashur and Pagma's Rare Book, and Khan's smithy with the production contest.
- **Economy.** Won, a starter debt, an account terminal and phone to work, sell equipment and buy, and apprentice blacksmiths you can hire.
- **Leveled mobs and elites** with rated loot and a boss, the Bone Lord.

### Notes
- Worlds created by development builds are not guaranteed to load. From this version on, the saved data of each player carries a version number, so later releases can migrate saves.
- The mod is an unofficial fan project; see the README.
